# Attendance and Archive Method

The live archive preserves a personal listening history. `event.status`
describes its radar state; `attendance.status` records the separate conclusion
about whether the show was attended.

## Evidence types

- `user_confirmed`
- `personal_photo`
- `ticket_purchase`
- `logistics_email`
- `calendar_or_planning`
- `third_party_ticket`
- `archival_reference`
- `post_event_reference`
- `unknown`

A direct `user_confirmed` record is sufficient to classify an event as
`attended`. Ticket purchase, planning, or logistics evidence supports research
but does not by itself prove attendance; someone else may have purchased a
ticket. Conversely, lack of email evidence is never proof that a show was not
attended.

Historical leads recovered from email research remain leads until their date,
venue, and show can be responsibly normalized. Do not discard them because
details are incomplete. Record evidence separately and preserve uncertainty.
