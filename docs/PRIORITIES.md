# Radar Priorities

Priority is editorial judgment, not a claim of universal artistic rank.

| Tier | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| S+ | Tsuyoshi Yamamoto performing in North America: immediate alert and exceptional travel priority. |
| S | Historically significant performance, major living jazz master, or rare circumstance unlikely to recur. |
| A+ | Exceptional Japanese jazz artist, major contemporary jazz performance, unusually strong lineup, or intimate appearance. |
| A | Strong recommendation in jazz, fusion, progressive, experimental, rock, or metal. |

## Geography

| Classification | Scope |
| --- | --- |
| Local | Washington, DC; Arlington; Alexandria; Bethesda and the immediate DC area. |
| Short trip | Baltimore. |
| Trip | Philadelphia, Newark/New Jersey, and New York City. |
| Special trip | A farther destination that genuinely warrants the travel. |

`trip_verdict` explains the recommendation for the individual event; it is not
derived mechanically from city or priority.

## Musical axes

Use tags sparingly to support the radar’s editorial filters: Japan / Tokyo,
Masters, Latin, Acoustic Jazz, Fusion / Prog, and Experimental / Metal. Tags
are discovery aids, not genre taxonomy for its own sake.
